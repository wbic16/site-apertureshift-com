About

Zap Listen Mode is the first on-ramp: a listen-and-tap companion to Zap Zoom Echo, made for children ages 0–5 playing with a grown-up on the same screen.

This game has no recorded audio. Read or sing each line aloud as its words light up — your voice is the soundtrack. The lines come from the song ZAP ZOOM ECHO! (Upgoer Remix) by wbic16, written in simple words only.

How to play: tap Listen and read or sing along as each word lights up. Then it’s your child’s turn — the next word to tap gently pulses. A wrong tap just wiggles. There are no scores, no timers, and nothing to lose.

It is one of the Roads to the Future: simple games that teach multidimensional thinking and the Singular language, for every age group.

Free play is dealt, not fixed: the order in which the game suggests the next line is shuffled for each instance, and finishing all 11 lines deals a fresh order for next time. The grown-up number picker can still open any line directly, and the rod always shows the 11 lines in song order.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap ever leaves this device. One number is stored on this device only: a counter that rises by one each time all 11 lines are finished, used to deal the next suggested order — it never leaves the device. Multiplayer means people on the same screen — no online play, no chat, no strangers.
