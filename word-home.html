    About us — the long version

    Word Home is one of the Roads to the Future games, built to teach two things at once: how to think in more dimensions than the world is about to demand of you, and Singular — Will Bickford’s language, drawn from his own archive of words.

    In Singular, every word lives somewhere. Each word has an address — nine numbers, written in three groups of three. A word’s address is the average of the addresses of every scroll that contains it: words that show up in many scrolls get placed precisely, words known to the archive but not yet placed wait at home — 1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1 — and words that appear nowhere sit at the far corner, 9.9.9/9.9.9/9.9.9.

    That averaging is holographic positioning: no single scroll decides where a word lives. Every scroll that holds it pulls a little, and the word settles where the pulls balance. It is why closeness on the map is closeness in the text — words that keep showing up in the same kinds of scrolls drift together, and the nearest-neighbor rounds measure that drift as a straight Euclidean distance through all nine numbers.

    Home isn’t nowhere — it’s the first address, where every unplaced word starts. The far corner isn’t exile either: the moment a scroll uses a word like quixotic, it starts moving toward its true home.

    One address as heights: echo, 16.7.7/7.7.9/5.6.6 — three groups of three.

    Two more roads are being built: Nine Numbers — moving one dimension at a time — and Zap Zoom Echo — the language taught by ear. Planned for apertureshift.com.

      Accessibility

      Big tap targets. Every brick you can tap is at least 56px tall, and the answer bricks are bigger still. Right and wrong are never shown by color alone — every answer also carries a ✓ or ✗ mark, correct answers get a solid outline and answers you missed get a dashed one, and the verdict is always spelled out in words. The whole game plays by keyboard: Tab to a brick, Enter to choose it. Motion on this page respects your device’s reduced-motion setting.

      Privacy

      No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap or type ever leaves this device. Scores live only while this page is open. Multiplayer is same-screen only: no online play, no chat, no strangers.
