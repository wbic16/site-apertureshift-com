About Neuralese

Phext is an address layer. Plain text says what; a phext coordinate says where. Every scroll, and every word inside it, can be named by an address — a word's address is the average of the addresses of the scrolls that hold it, so meaning is positioned by the company it keeps.

Neuralese is what happens next: when both ends of a channel share the same atlas, you don't have to transmit the text at all — you transmit addresses instead. An address names a word exactly, in nine numbers.

The break-even rule is the heart of this game. Establishing a word in the channel's dictionary costs 10 tokens the first time — the 9 numbers of its address, plus the word itself. After that, each use costs 1 token. A dictionary entry therefore costs 9 extra numbers up front, and it pays off only when the word repeats enough across the channel's whole life — not within one scroll. Feel how long that payback really takes.

The Library is the deeper trick. A scroll both ends already hold, word for word, can be sent as a single scroll coordinate: 10 tokens flat, any length. That is compression with no trick in it — the bits never travel because both minds already have them. But a coordinate is a pointer, not a copy: if static garbles it, there is nothing to repair from, and the whole scroll must be resent as full text. Pointers are cheap and fragile; copies are costly and sturdy. Choosing between them, send by send, is the game.

Par is the cheapest safe play for each scroll, computed honestly: full text (or dictionary packets for words already established), the flat coordinate on Library scrolls when the channel is clean, and never a coordinate where a garble would make it the expensive option.

Matches are dealt, not fixed. Each match is dealt by a generator seeded with the current ISO week and an on-device generation counter: the scroll order is shuffled, and which 2 of the 5 rounds carry the static is drawn fresh — never announced before your choice locks. Completing a match advances the generation, so the next match is a new deal, and the instance line at the top names the exact deal you are playing. Because dictionary amortization depends on order, par is recomputed at deal time over the dealt order and the dealt garble rounds — it is a property of this instance, not a fixed number. Restarting mid-match re-deals the identical instance; in a duel, both players face the same deal with their own dictionaries.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing leaves this device. The only thing stored is the match generation counter, kept only on this device, in this browser, so each completed match deals a fresh instance. Multiplayer is same-screen only: no online play, no chat, no strangers.
