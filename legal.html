Privacy, Terms & License

Privacy

Roads to the Future games collect nothing.

  • No accounts and no sign-up. We never ask who you are.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • The games run entirely in your browser. Nothing you tap, type, or score is sent anywhere; scores exist only while the page is open and are gone when you close it.
  • Multiplayer is same-screen only — people playing together on one device. There is no online play, no chat, no messaging, and no contact with strangers, for players of any age.
  • This website's host may keep ordinary server logs (like every website); the games themselves send the host nothing beyond the page request.

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to sell, share, or leak. That is the design, not just the policy.

Terms of play

  • Free. The games are free to play, forever. There are no purchases, no subscriptions, no in-game sales, and no ads — and there is no paid version.
  • For everyone. The games are suitable for all ages and designed to be played together.
  • As-is. The games are provided as-is, without warranties. If something breaks, tell us and we will try to fix it.
