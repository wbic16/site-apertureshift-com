    About Kardashev Climb

    The Kardashev scale measures a civilisation by the power it can use. The formula is K = (log10 P − 6) / 10, with P in watts. A home ranch cluster at 4 kW sits at K ≈ −0.24 — below zero, off the bottom of the famous scale. The Exocortex program's checkpoints are 1 MW (K = 0) by 2037, 1016 W (K = 1.0) by 2083, 1021 W (K = 1.5) by 2106, and 1026 W (K = 2.0) by 2130.

    This game teaches the one skill the climb runs on: telling a rate from a base. Building nodes raises the base once. Efficiency raises the rate forever. Contracts buy time with attention. Cooling keeps the hardware alive — the ranch rule is a sustained ≤75°C, and this game's Heat gauge is that rule with the numbers filed off.

    Honest accounting: “AGI on 2 kilowatts” is a small lie — the real system also burns cloud tokens. Local watts are the ranch's share; most inference brains draw unmetered datacenter power behind flat subscriptions. This game counts local watts only, and says so.
