About Runway

The premise. You start with a $20 loan from a friend who believes in the work. Turn it into runway. Profits are yours; only revenue up to the agreed limit is owed back. Nothing illegal, no gambling. A day job covers your living costs exactly and adds $150 a month of experiment money — everything past that, you have to earn.

The runway doctrine:

  • Revenue drivers and feedback loops beat pedigree. Nobody funds a background. They fund what comes back.
  • Sell receipts and artifacts, never a resume. A finished thing you can point at out-argues every claim about yourself.
  • A channel is not real until it names names. A partner with a client book is a rumor until three actual candidates are on the table.
  • Never sell what does not exist yet. The Solar Pod takes no outreach until it is 100% built. Selling air costs the only currency that compounds: reputation.
  • One focus a month. Attention is the constraint. The day job pays the rent; the focus builds the road out.
  • Only cash received counts. Promises, pipelines, and applause are not income. The monthly statement names every dollar by its source.

How the gauges work. Cash is cash. Runway (the Reserve) starts at 1 month and grows by a month for every $1,000 banked — reach 12 months of runway by month 24 to win. Skill XP levels up your levers: faster builds, better consulting rates. Reputation (0–100) gates the consulting pipeline — diagnostics only land at Reputation 30+. Small Flips only pay on verified ≥8× comps; blind buying is a slow bleed by design.

Anti-viral mechanics — the game adjusts after every solution. Completing a run increments an on-device generation counter. Each new run is dealt from a seed made of the current ISO week plus that generation, shown on every statement as Run YYYY-Www · G<n> · seed XXXX. The event deck order and which items appear on the Small Flips candidate table are drawn from that seed, so a memorised solution does not replay. What does not change: flip prices stay inside the verified bands and only a verified ≥8× comp can pay — that rule is a fact, not an instance — and the doctrine gates, the prices of record ($250 diagnostic, $1,250 sprint, $49/site/mo), and the statement format never change. In Two Builders, both ledgers face the same dealt month sequence from the same session seed.

XP: finishing all 18 Roads to the Future games grants you disk space on edit.phext.io as a Volumen-class Wavefront grant. Historic entries are all Incipit-class grants.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing leaves this device. Runs are stored only on this device, in this browser. Multiplayer is same-screen only.

The game saves your current run and your best results in this browser’s local storage, on this device only, so a run can survive a closed tab. No name, no identifier, and no play history is ever transmitted anywhere — there is no server for it to go to.

It also keeps a simple generation counter on this device only: each completed run raises it by one, and the next run’s seed (ISO week + generation) is dealt from it. The counter is a number, not an identifier — it is never transmitted, and erasing below resets it.
