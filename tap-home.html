About

Everything in the Exocortex lives somewhere. Every word, every memory, every idea sits at an address made of nine numbers — three groups of three, like 1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1.

That address is home — the very first address, where everything starts. This game is a first touch of that idea, made for the smallest hands: tap the big home brick, and a real word pops up at its real address, drawn as nine bars so little eyes can see that some numbers are tall and some are small.

The words and addresses here are real. They come from Will Bickford’s own archive of words, mapped into the Exocortex. When your child is older, the same addresses wait for them in the bigger games — the road goes all the way to 2130.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap ever leaves this device. Nothing is stored. Multiplayer means people on the same screen — there is no online play, no chat, no strangers.
