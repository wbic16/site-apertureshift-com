About

In 2130, every word, memory, and task lives at an address of nine numbers — three groups of three, like 13.8.15/11.9.12/8.8.8. That system is called phext, and it was built the way you play this game: one dimension at a time. Learn to walk the nine numbers here, and you can find anything in the Exocortex — and never lose it.

How the road works

  1. You start at 1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1 — home, the root of everything.
  2. A target address appears. Step your numbers up or down, one number at a time, until yours matches it exactly. Numbers never go below 1.
  3. A shelf lights up and gets a check mark when all three of its numbers are right, and each step tells you warmer or colder.
  4. Each road has a par — the fewest steps possible. Match par and you earn three stars. Hold a stepper button down to keep stepping.

Ways to play

  • 1 Player — walk the eight roads on your own, then the endless Open Road.
  • 2 Players — Race — two navigators, Red and Blue, race to the same target on the same screen. First to match it wins the road; whoever wins the most roads after eight is crowned.
  • 2 Players — Together — one shared address. Red and Blue take turns making one step at a time, and the team scores together against par.

Accessibility

Built with big tap targets. Progress and matches are never shown by color alone — matched shelves also get a check mark.

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap or type ever leaves this device. Scores live only while this page is open. Multiplayer is same-screen only: no online play, no chat, no strangers.
