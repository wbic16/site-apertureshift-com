About One Number Road

For grown-ups. Every idea in the Exocortex lives at an address — a list of numbers, like the address of a house, but with as many numbers as the idea needs. Nine Numbers, the next game on this road, uses all nine numbers of an address.

One Number Road is dimension one of Nine Numbers: just an address’s first number, learned as a road you roll along. There is one wheel, one cart, and one number to find. Rolling forward adds one; rolling back takes one away; the number can never drop below 1, because 1 is home — the start of every address.

How to play together: one child rolls, or two children share the screen. The “Pass the wheel” brick hands the turn to the other color — Red and Blue each roll their own roads, and each child’s finished roads simply pile up as filled joints. There is no score against the child, no timer, and no way to fail: a road is done when the cart arrives, however many rolls it takes.

When rolling to 5 on the Longer Road is easy, your child is ready for the next on-ramp: Nine Numbers, where the wheel becomes nine wheels and the road becomes an address.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap ever leaves this device. One number is stored on this device only: a generation counter that re-deals the roads once every road has been rolled. Multiplayer means people on the same screen — no online play, no chat, no strangers.
