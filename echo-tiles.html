About — for grown-ups

Echo Tiles is a first-words game for ages 3–5. Its six lines come from ZAP ZOOM ECHO!, a song written in simple words only — big ideas don't need big words.

The finished line always stays visible at the top, because this game is about touching words in order, not about memory. There are no scores and no timers, and a wrong tile costs nothing — it just wiggles and waits to be tried again.

Turn on Take turns and two players share one screen: Red and Blue place tiles one at a time and celebrate together.

When a player is ready for more, the next road is Zap Zoom Echo — the same song, all eleven lines, with stars to earn.

Privacy

No accounts. No sign-up. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you tap ever leaves this device. Multiplayer means people on the same screen — no online play, no chat, no strangers. The only thing stored, on this device only, is a generation counter that re-deals the extra tiles in rounds 4–6.
