# apertureshift.com — Roads to the Future

Eighteen free games that teach multidimensional thinking and the Singular
language, one road for every age from 0 to 20:

- **0–2 · First Taps** — `tap-home.html`, `zap-listen-mode.html`
- **3–5 · First Bricks** — `sort-the-scrolls.html`, `one-number-road.html`, `echo-tiles.html`
- **6–9 · Roads and Atlas** — `nine-numbers.html`, `word-home.html`, `zap-zoom-echo.html`
- **10–12 · Builders** — `scroll-seeker.html`, `place-a-word.html`
- **13–15 · Systems** — `kardashev-climb.html`, `find-it-fast.html`, `exocortex-quest-i.html`
- **16–17 · Mechanism and Meaning** — `neuralese.html`, `the-long-road.html`
- **18–20 · The Hand-off** — `your-own-archive.html`, `runway.html`, `the-choir.html`

The hub (`index.html`) carries the XP Road Card: finish all eighteen games
to earn a Volumen-class Wavefront grant of disk space on edit.phext.io.
The design of record lives in `GRANT-PROCESS.md` (the grant) and
`ANTI-VIRAL.md` (why the games re-deal themselves after every solution).

All games: free forever, same-screen multiplayer, no sign-up, no ads,
no tracking — nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
Open `index.html` in a browser. MIT licensed — see `LICENSE`.
